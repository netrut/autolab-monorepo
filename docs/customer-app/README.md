# 📱 AutoLab Customer App — Complete Documentation

> **Location:** `apps/customer_app/`  
> **Stack:** Flutter (same as `apps/flutter-app/`)  
> **Backend:** Shared backend at `apps/backend/` (same APIs, role-based access)

---

## 📌 Purpose

The Customer App allows vehicle owners to:
- Register & manage their vehicles
- View service history & invoices generated by service centres
- Book services at nearby service centres
- Search & discover service centres
- Share vehicle access with family/friends
- Receive notifications & reminders for upcoming services
- Fill basic service forms (without invoice generation)

## 🔑 Key Differences from Service Centre App (`flutter-app`)

| Feature | Service Centre App | Customer App |
|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| Service Form | Full form + invoice generation | View-only / basic form (no invoice) |
| Service Centre Management | Create/manage own centre | Search & browse centres |
| Team Members | Manage staff | N/A |
| Invoice | Generate & share | View received invoices |
| Vehicle Access | Service vehicles | Own vehicles + shared access |
| Bookings | Receive & manage | Create & track |
| Home Screen | Service-centre focused | Customer-vehicle focused |
