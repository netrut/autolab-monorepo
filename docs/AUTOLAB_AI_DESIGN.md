🎯 AUTOLAB AI DESIGN PROMPT (FINAL PROFESSIONAL VERSION)

Design a clean, structured, minimal automotive service management mobile application UI named “AUTOLAB”, developed in Flutter.

The app purpose is to provide a centralized digital vehicle service history and reminder system for both customers and service centres.

The design should follow principles inspired by:

CRED app (clean spacing, structured hierarchy, calm typography)

Kia / Hyundai official apps (organized vehicle cards, grouped information blocks, soft rounded cards)

Avoid:

Techy neon UI

Glassmorphism

Heavy gradients

Over decorative visuals

The UI should feel:
Professional, trustworthy, automotive corporate, minimal, clean, scalable.

🧩 PRODUCT PURPOSE

AUTOLAB is used by:

Customers

Track vehicle service history

See due & upcoming services

Maintain oil change details, parts replaced, etc.

Receive reminders

Add four-wheeler and two-wheeler

Service Centres

Search vehicle by number

Update service details

Mark parts replaced

Enter oil & service details

Set next service date

Notify customer

This is a vehicle service record management system.
--

Design Requirements:

• Clean white or light grey background
• Minimal and corporate automotive style
• No gradients, no neon effects, no heavy illustrations
• Use soft rounded cards (12dp radius)
• Light shadows
• Clear spacing system (8dp grid)
• Inspired by Kia and Hyundai official service apps
• Professional utility app look
